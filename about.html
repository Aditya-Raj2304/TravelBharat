About

A simple guide to India's destinations

TravelBharat gathers destination information in one place, organised by state and city.

Our aim is an accessible travel guide that helps people discover India's heritage, landscapes and cultures through one easy-to-use site.

Mission and vision

To make destination discovery in India simple, visual and structured, so travellers can compare places within a region without opening dozens of tabs.

How destinations are organised

  • States and union territories, grouped by region
  • Cities and localities within each state
  • Categories such as heritage, nature, spiritual, adventure and beach

Built with

  • HTML5 for structure, CSS3 for design, vanilla JavaScript for behaviour
  • Local data in js/data.js
  • No frameworks and no backend
