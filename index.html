Good to know

Good to know before you go

About TravelBharat
When is the best time to visit?

It depends on the region. Many northern and western places are most comfortable from October to March, while hill and Himalayan areas have their own seasons. Each destination page lists a best time.

Do I need permits for any destination?

Some border and protected areas need permits. Rules change, so check with official tourism sources before you travel.

How should I dress at temples and churches?

Dress modestly with covered shoulders and knees, and be ready to remove your shoes at many temples.

Are road and pass conditions reliable year-round?

Not in the mountains. Snow and rain can close roads, so check current conditions close to your travel date.

How accurate is the information here?

The content is sample data for this first version. Treat it as inspiration and confirm timings and conditions with official sources.
